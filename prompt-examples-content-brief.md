# Content Brief

## Reusable prompt

> Help me prepare a content brief based on the context provided. Ask for missing essentials, organize the output into useful sections, and list concrete next actions.

## Context fields
- Purpose and audience
- Background and constraints
- Source information
- Desired format and deadline

## Quality checks
- Be specific and concise.
- Separate known facts from assumptions.
- Note important gaps.
- Make actions measurable where possible.
