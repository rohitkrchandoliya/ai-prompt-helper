# Team Change Plan

## Reusable prompt

> Help me prepare a team change plan using the context I provide. Ask for essential missing details, distinguish evidence from assumptions, organize the response clearly, and include actionable next steps.

## Context to provide
- Goal and audience
- Background and constraints
- Available source material
- Preferred format and length
- Timeline and success criteria

## Quality check
- Keep claims grounded in the provided evidence.
- Identify unknowns and limitations.
- Make next steps specific and verifiable.
