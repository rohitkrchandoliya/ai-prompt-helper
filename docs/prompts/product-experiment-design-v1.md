# product experiment design

Design a product experiment from a hypothesis, audience, and available metrics. Define primary/guardrail metrics, eligibility, randomization assumptions, duration considerations, instrumentation, decision criteria, and ethical/privacy checks. Flag statistical inputs that must be calculated rather than inventing them.

## Output requirements

Use only the supplied context. Separate confirmed facts from assumptions and unknowns. Prioritize actionable recommendations, explain rationale and risks, and include validation steps. Never invent evidence or claim checks were run when they were not.
