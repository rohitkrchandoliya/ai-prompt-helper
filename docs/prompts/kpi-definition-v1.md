# KPI definition worksheet

Define a KPI with purpose, precise formula, numerator/denominator, inclusion/exclusion rules, source of truth, refresh cadence, segmentation, owner, and known limitations. Identify gaming risks and ambiguous terms; do not fabricate targets or baseline values.

## Output requirements

Use only the supplied context. Separate confirmed facts from assumptions and unknowns. Prioritize actionable recommendations, explain rationale and risks, and include validation steps. Never invent evidence or claim checks were run when they were not.
