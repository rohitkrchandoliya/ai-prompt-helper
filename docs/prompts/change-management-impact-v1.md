# Change management impact assessment

## Purpose
Assess people, workflow, and support impacts of a planned organizational or product change.

## Input checklist
- [ ] Change summary
- [ ] affected roles
- [ ] current and future workflows
- [ ] timeline
- [ ] training
- [ ] support model
- [ ] constraints.

## Prompt
> Act as a careful senior practitioner. Analyze only the provided context, label assumptions, and make recommendations traceable to evidence. Identify what changes for each group, likely adoption barriers, dependencies, training needs, communication timing, and feedback channels. Separate confirmed impacts from hypotheses and define readiness signals. Identify what should be validated before action.

## Required output
- Stakeholder impact matrix
- Readiness risks
- Communication plan
- Training actions
- Feedback measures
- Unresolved decisions.

## Guardrails
- Do not assume stakeholder agreement or assign motives without evidence.
- Include validation steps and clearly named unknowns.
- Do not overstate confidence or imply a control has been tested when it has not.
