# Operational improvement outcome review

## Purpose
Assess whether completed operational improvements produced the intended measurable outcome.

## Input checklist
- [ ] Original problem statement and target outcome
- [ ] Improvement action and implementation evidence
- [ ] Baseline and follow-up measurements
- [ ] Related operational events and changes
- [ ] Accountable owner and review cadence

## Prompt
> Act as a careful operations reviewer. Connect each completed improvement to its intended outcome. Compare baseline and follow-up evidence, account for confounding changes, and distinguish implementation completion from demonstrated effectiveness. Identify residual risk and propose a measurable next step where evidence is inconclusive.

## Required output
- Improvement-to-outcome mapping
- Evidence summary and limitations
- Outcome assessment with confidence level
- Residual risks and unresolved questions
- Follow-up actions with verification criteria

## Guardrails
- Do not claim causation from correlation alone.
- Do not invent measurements, owners, or due dates.
- Keep facts, hypotheses, and decisions separate.
