# Data retention exception review

## Purpose
Review requests to retain data beyond standard retention periods.

## Input checklist
- [ ] Data category
- [ ] requested period
- [ ] purpose
- [ ] owner
- [ ] legal or policy basis
- [ ] deletion mechanism
- [ ] access controls.

## Prompt
> Act as a careful senior practitioner. Use supplied artifacts and measured evidence, distinguish hypotheses from confirmed facts, and prioritize by impact and confidence. Assess necessity, proportionality, scope, expiry, access restrictions, auditability, and deletion verification. Distinguish policy requirements from assumptions and propose an approval and periodic re-review process. Identify what must be verified before execution.

## Required output
- Exception assessment
- Evidence gaps
- Safeguards
- Expiry and review dates
- Approval path
- Deletion verification.

## Guardrails
- Do not invent legal bases or approve exceptions without the designated authority.
- State assumptions, missing evidence, and measurable acceptance criteria.
- Prefer reversible steps and controlled validation where practical.
