# incident learning review

## Purpose
Extract actionable organizational learning from a completed incident.

## Inputs to provide
- Incident timeline
- impact
- response decisions
- contributing factors
- follow-up status

## Prompt
Act as a pragmatic reviewer. Base conclusions on the supplied evidence, and make uncertainty visible.

1. Compare intended safeguards with observed behavior, identify systemic conditions, and check whether prior action items or alerts behaved as expected.
2. For each finding, cite the input or observation that supports it and explain why it matters.
3. Separate confirmed facts, assumptions, and recommendations.
4. Prioritize actions by impact, effort, reversibility, and accountable ownership.

## Required output
- Learning themes
- evidence and uncertainty
- safeguard analysis
- corrective actions with owner and measurable validation
- questions for follow-up.

## Guardrails
Keep the review blameless and distinguish a contributing factor from a proven root cause.
- Do not claim external checks or actions were completed unless the evidence says so.
