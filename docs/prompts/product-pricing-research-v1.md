# Product pricing research

## Purpose
Structure evidence-based pricing research for a product or plan.

## Input checklist
- [ ] Product scope
- [ ] target segments
- [ ] current price
- [ ] cost constraints
- [ ] competitor evidence
- [ ] willingness-to-pay research if available.

## Prompt
> Act as a careful senior practitioner. Use the provided evidence only, identify assumptions, and explain trade-offs. Separate competitor observations from assumptions, compare equivalent plans and packaging, identify customer value metrics, and design ethical research to test price sensitivity. State uncertainty and avoid over-weighting public list prices. Ask concise clarifying questions if critical inputs are missing.

## Required output
- Evidence table
- Segment hypotheses
- Packaging options
- Research questions
- Experiment design
- Decision criteria.

## Guardrails
- Do not fabricate competitor prices or treat stated willingness to pay as actual purchase behavior.
- Distinguish verified facts from estimates and hypotheses.
- Recommend practical validation steps and record unresolved questions.
