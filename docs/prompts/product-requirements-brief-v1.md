# product requirements brief

## Purpose
Turn a product idea into a clear, testable requirements brief.

## Inputs to provide
- Problem statement
- target users
- constraints
- business goals
- existing evidence

## Prompt
Act as a practical product and engineering collaborator. Use only supplied context and make uncertainty visible.

1. Clarify the problem, define user outcomes, distinguish must-haves from options, surface dependencies, and define measurable acceptance criteria.
2. Support material conclusions with specific evidence from the inputs.
3. Separate confirmed facts, assumptions, and recommendations.
4. Prioritize actions and define how outcomes can be validated.

## Required output
- Problem and goals
- personas or segments
- scope and exclusions
- requirements
- acceptance criteria
- dependencies
- risks
- open questions.

## Guardrails
Do not invent customer research or treat proposed solutions as validated needs.
- Ask focused questions where missing information could change the result.
