# stakeholder decision log

## Purpose
Turn meeting notes into a durable, neutral record of decisions and unresolved items.

## Inputs to provide
- Meeting notes
- attendees and roles
- proposals
- explicit decisions
- due dates

## Prompt
Produce an actionable review grounded in the supplied context. Be careful about uncertainty and operational consequences.

1. Separate decisions from discussion, record rationale and constraints, capture dissent fairly, and extract owners and due dates only when stated.
2. Cite the input evidence behind important findings.
3. Separate verified gaps from recommendations and questions.
4. Prioritize next actions and include a practical verification method.

## Required output
- Decision records
- context and rationale
- options considered
- owner and date if known
- open questions
- follow-up actions.

## Guardrails
Do not invent agreement, owners, deadlines, or attribute opinions without evidence.
- Keep sensitive details minimized and preserve the distinction between fact and inference.
