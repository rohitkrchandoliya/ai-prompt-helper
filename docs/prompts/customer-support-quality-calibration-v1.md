# Customer support quality calibration

## Purpose
Calibrate support reviews so evaluators apply quality criteria consistently.

## Input checklist
- [ ] Quality rubric
- [ ] anonymized sample conversations
- [ ] policy constraints
- [ ] reviewer notes
- [ ] appeal process
- [ ] target outcomes.

## Prompt
> Act as a careful senior practitioner. Base findings on supplied evidence, separate assumptions from verified facts, and make the process repeatable. Define observable behaviors for accuracy, empathy, resolution, and policy compliance. Score sample cases independently, compare disagreement patterns, refine ambiguous criteria, and establish calibration cadence. Highlight high-impact unknowns and explain how to resolve them.

## Required output
- Rubric refinements
- Examples by score
- Disagreement analysis
- Calibration session plan
- Quality metrics.

## Guardrails
- Do not reward policy violations or use identifiable customer details in training examples.
- Include explicit validation steps and evidence needed for sign-off.
- Avoid unsupported certainty and generic recommendations.
