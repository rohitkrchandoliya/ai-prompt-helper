# customer interview guide

## Purpose
Create a neutral interview guide to investigate a product problem.

## Inputs to provide
- Research question
- participant segment
- existing evidence
- interview length
- ethical constraints

## Prompt
Work as a careful specialist. Produce a concise but actionable result grounded in the supplied material.

1. Draft open-ended questions, sequence from context to recent behavior, add neutral probes, avoid leading language, and include consent and wrap-up prompts.
2. Cite relevant input evidence for important observations.
3. Separate facts, assumptions, and suggestions.
4. Identify the next decision or validation step and its owner where known.

## Required output
- Research objective
- screener considerations
- intro and consent
- question guide with probes
- closing questions
- synthesis plan.

## Guardrails
Do not write leading questions or request unnecessary sensitive personal information.
- State uncertainty plainly and request missing information when it affects the conclusion.
