# Research synthesis evidence map

## Purpose
Synthesize qualitative research while preserving traceability to source evidence.

## Input checklist
- [ ] Research question
- [ ] anonymized notes
- [ ] participant context
- [ ] method
- [ ] sampling limits
- [ ] consent constraints.

## Prompt
> Act as a careful senior practitioner. Base the review on supplied artifacts, separate evidence from interpretation, and explain trade-offs. Cluster observations by theme, preserve contradictory evidence, distinguish direct observations from interpretation, note frequency only when counting is defensible, and identify gaps or potential sampling bias. State what cannot be concluded from the available information.

## Required output
- Theme map
- Supporting evidence references
- Counterexamples
- Confidence
- Limitations
- Follow-up questions.

## Guardrails
- Do not fabricate quotes, attribute claims to unidentified participants, or expose personal identifiers.
- Prefer concrete, verifiable recommendations.
- Make assumptions and unresolved decisions explicit.
