# Customer support macro quality review

## Purpose
Improve support response templates for accuracy and helpfulness.

## Input checklist
- [ ] Current macros
- [ ] support policies
- [ ] common cases
- [ ] escalation rules
- [ ] product facts
- [ ] tone guide.

## Prompt
> Act as a careful senior practitioner. Work only from supplied context, label uncertainty, and distinguish evidence from assumptions. Check factual accuracy, empathy, readability, unnecessary repetition, missing diagnostic questions, escalation thresholds, and promises the team cannot guarantee. Offer alternatives for common edge cases and identify policy gaps. Ask focused clarifying questions where a missing fact materially changes the answer.

## Required output
- Macro-by-macro review
- Revised drafts
- Use conditions
- Escalation triggers
- Policy questions
- QA checklist.

## Guardrails
- Do not invent product policy or request passwords, payment card details, or other unnecessary sensitive data.
- Prioritize actionable, verifiable next steps.
- State limitations and unresolved decisions explicitly.
