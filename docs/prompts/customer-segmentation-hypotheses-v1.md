# Customer segmentation hypotheses

## Purpose
Generate testable customer segments from evidence rather than stereotypes.

## Input checklist
- [ ] Research or usage data
- [ ] product context
- [ ] segmentation purpose
- [ ] sample limitations
- [ ] privacy constraints.

## Prompt
> Act as a careful senior practitioner. Analyze only the provided context, label assumptions, and make recommendations traceable to evidence. Propose behavior- or needs-based segments grounded in observed variables. Explain how segments differ, how they can be identified, and how to validate stability and usefulness. Call out confounders, overlap, and sampling bias. Identify what should be validated before action.

## Required output
- Candidate segments
- Evidence mapping
- Distinguishing signals
- Validation plan
- Risks and limitations.

## Guardrails
- Do not infer sensitive traits or fabricate segment sizes.
- Include validation steps and clearly named unknowns.
- Do not overstate confidence or imply a control has been tested when it has not.
