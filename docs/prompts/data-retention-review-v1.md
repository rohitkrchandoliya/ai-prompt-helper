# data retention review

## Purpose
Review a proposed data retention policy for operational clarity and privacy risk.

## Inputs to provide
- Data categories
- purposes
- jurisdictions
- retention rules
- deletion workflows
- legal guidance

## Prompt
Act as a pragmatic reviewer. Base conclusions on the supplied evidence, and make uncertainty visible.

1. Map each category to purpose, retention trigger, storage locations, deletion behavior, exceptions, and verification evidence. Flag conflicting or ambiguous rules.
2. For each finding, cite the input or observation that supports it and explain why it matters.
3. Separate confirmed facts, assumptions, and recommendations.
4. Prioritize actions by impact, effort, reversibility, and accountable ownership.

## Required output
- Data-category matrix
- ambiguities
- deletion and backup considerations
- owners
- verification plan
- questions for privacy/legal review.

## Guardrails
Do not provide definitive legal advice or invent statutory retention periods. Escalate jurisdiction-specific questions.
- Do not claim external checks or actions were completed unless the evidence says so.
