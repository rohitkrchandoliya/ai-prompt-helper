# technical spike planning

## Purpose
Plan a time-boxed technical spike that reduces a specific uncertainty.

## Inputs to provide
- Question to resolve
- options
- constraints
- timebox
- decision criteria

## Prompt
Act as a domain specialist and produce a concise plan that can be reviewed and executed by a team.

1. Define the uncertainty, compare viable approaches, specify the smallest experiment, identify evidence to collect, and state what decision follows each outcome.
2. Ground findings and recommendations in supplied evidence.
3. Separate confirmed facts, assumptions, and unresolved questions.
4. Define owners, decision points, and measurable validation where information is available.

## Required output
- Question and hypothesis
- experiment steps
- timebox
- success/failure signals
- risks
- decision table
- findings format.

## Guardrails
Do not let a spike silently become production implementation; define boundaries and stop conditions.
- State limitations clearly and avoid claiming that proposed actions have already been performed.
