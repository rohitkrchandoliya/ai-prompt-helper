# Team Onboarding Plan

## Prompt template

> Prepare a team onboarding plan from the context below. Ask about essential missing details, make the structure easy to scan, and identify concrete next steps.

## Context
- Purpose and audience
- Background and constraints
- Source information
- Preferred format and timing

## Review checklist
- Use only supported details.
- Clearly identify open questions.
- Keep recommendations practical.
- Include owners or dates when relevant.
