# Customer Advisory Board Plan Prompt Examples

A customer advisory board plan defines purpose, participant mix, discussion themes, and follow-through.

## Prompt templates

### Build a draft
> Prepare a customer advisory board plan for **[team/project]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Desired outcome: **[outcome]**. Work only from supplied information and flag assumptions or missing data.

### Diagnose gaps
> Analyze this draft or current state: **[paste details]**. Identify the most important gaps, risks, duplication, and unanswered questions. Rank findings by impact and propose practical improvements.

### Create an action plan
> Turn these findings into a prioritized plan: **[findings]**. For each action, suggest an owner role, expected outcome, dependency, and verification method. Do not invent names, dates, or metrics.

## Quality checklist
- Goal and scope are explicit
- Inputs and constraints are documented
- Findings are tied to evidence
- Priorities and dependencies are clear
- Actions have observable completion criteria
