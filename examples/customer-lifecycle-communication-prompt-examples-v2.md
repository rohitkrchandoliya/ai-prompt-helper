# Customer Lifecycle Communication Prompt Examples

A lifecycle communication plan adapts messages to customer needs at onboarding, adoption, renewal, and other relevant stages.

## Prompt templates

### Draft a lifecycle map
> Create a customer lifecycle communication map for **[product/context]**. Audience segments: **[segments]**. Goals: **[goals]**. Use supplied facts and identify missing information.

### Review messages
> Review these messages for clarity, timing, relevance, accessibility, and consistency: **[messages]**. Identify confusing or repetitive content and suggest improvements.

### Plan measurement
> Recommend a proportionate way to evaluate whether each message helps customers take the intended next step. Identify suitable signals, limitations, and follow-up questions without inventing benchmarks.

## Quality checklist
- Each message has a clear purpose
- Timing and audience are appropriate
- Claims are accurate and qualified
- Customer preferences are respected
- Measurement limitations are documented
