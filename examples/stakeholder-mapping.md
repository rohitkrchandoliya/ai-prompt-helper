# Stakeholder Mapping Prompt Examples

## Map stakeholders
"Create an initial stakeholder map for [project]. Group stakeholders by role, decision authority, impact, information needs, and likely engagement. Mark inferred interests as hypotheses to validate, not facts."

## Plan engagement
"Build a stakeholder engagement plan with audience, objective, message, channel, timing, owner role, feedback route, and success signal. Include groups that may be overlooked and identify accessibility or language needs to confirm."

## Resolve competing needs
"Summarize these stakeholder needs and identify agreements, conflicts, dependencies, and unanswered questions. Suggest a fair process for prioritizing them using the project's stated goals and constraints. Notes: [paste]."
