# Volunteer Coordination Prompt Examples

## 1. Write a task brief
> Turn these event needs [needs] into a volunteer task brief with purpose, responsibilities, estimated duration, required skills, supplies, and a contact role. Mark unknown logistics instead of making them up.

## 2. Prepare a shift handover
> Organize these handover notes [notes] into completed work, pending tasks, safety considerations, supplies, and escalation contacts. Exclude personal information that the next volunteer does not need.

## 3. Summarize event follow-up
> Based on these event notes [notes], draft a follow-up summary with outcomes, unresolved tasks, lessons, and next steps. Distinguish confirmed counts from estimates and do not invent impact metrics.
