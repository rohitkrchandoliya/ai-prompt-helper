# Executive Summary Writing Prompt Examples

## Summarize a report
"Write an executive summary of this report for [decision-making audience]. Lead with the main finding, decision needed, material evidence, implications, risks, and next steps. Preserve caveats and do not introduce facts absent from the source. Report: [paste]."

## Adjust summary length
"Condense this summary to [word count] words while retaining the conclusion, strongest supporting evidence, key limitation, and requested action. List any important nuance that could not fit. Summary: [paste]."

## Check executive readiness
"Review this executive summary for clarity, decision relevance, unsupported claims, jargon, and missing trade-offs. Suggest precise edits and identify questions the author must resolve. Summary: [paste]."
