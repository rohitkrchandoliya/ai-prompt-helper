# Customer Journey Workshop Facilitation Prompt Examples

A customer journey workshop helps teams map user needs, moments of friction, opportunities, and evidence gaps.

## Prompt templates

### Draft
> Draft a customer journey workshop facilitation for **[product/team/context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Goal: **[goal]**. Use supplied information, state assumptions, and mark missing inputs.

### Improve
> Review this draft for clarity, sequence, completeness, accessibility, and practical usefulness: **[draft]**. Identify important gaps and provide an improved version.

### Validate
> Create a short validation checklist for the revised material. Include realistic scenarios, edge cases, user questions, and clear criteria for completion.

## Quality checklist
- Purpose and audience are explicit
- Steps are easy to follow
- Terms are consistent
- Assumptions and limits are visible
- Validation criteria are actionable
