# Vendor Comparison Prompt Examples

## 1. Define comparison criteria
> Based on this need [need], propose vendor evaluation criteria with importance and evidence requirements. Separate must-haves from preferences and flag criteria requiring stakeholder agreement.

## 2. Compare proposals
> Compare these vendor proposals [proposal details] against the same criteria [criteria]. Cite the supplied evidence, identify missing information, and avoid scoring unavailable data as fact.

## 3. Prepare vendor questions
> From these gaps [gaps], draft neutral questions for vendors about scope, service levels, security, accessibility, support, costs, and exit terms as relevant. Do not assume contractual commitments.
