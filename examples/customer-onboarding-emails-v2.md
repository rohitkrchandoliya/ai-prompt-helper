# Customer Onboarding Email Sequence Prompt Examples

An onboarding email sequence guides users through relevant first steps without overwhelming them.

## Prompt templates

### Draft

### Critique
> Critique this draft for clarity, sequence, completeness, inclusivity, and practicality: **[draft]**. Prioritize improvements and provide an edited version.

### Apply

## Quality checklist
- Audience and purpose are clear
- Important constraints are considered
- Assumptions are stated
- Actions and responsibilities are explicit
- Success checks are observable
