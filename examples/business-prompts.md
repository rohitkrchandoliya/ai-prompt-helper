# Business Prompt Examples

## Clarify a value proposition
Help define a value proposition for [product/service] aimed at [audience]. Identify the audience's likely problem, the proposed benefit, alternatives, and assumptions that need validation. Do not invent customer research.

## Write a decision memo
Prepare a concise decision memo about [decision]. Include context, options, evaluation criteria, trade-offs, recommendation, risks, and the next decision point. Separate known facts from estimates.

## Plan customer discovery
Draft 8 neutral interview questions for people who may use [product/service]. Avoid leading questions and sales language. For each question, state what assumption it helps explore.
