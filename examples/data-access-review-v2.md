# Data Access Review Prompt Examples

A data access review checks whether access aligns with roles, approved purposes, and documented review criteria.

## Prompt templates

### Build
> Build a data access review for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Goal: **[goal]**. Use the given evidence, call out missing inputs, and keep recommendations proportionate.

### Review
> Review this draft for completeness, clarity, consistency, and risk: **[draft]**. Identify the most consequential gaps and suggest precise improvements.

### Operationalize
> Turn the recommended approach into a plan with steps, role-based owners, dependencies, verification, and unresolved questions. Do not invent unknown facts.

## Checklist
- Scope and outcome are explicit
- Evidence and assumptions are separated
- Risks and dependencies are documented
- Actions have clear verification
- Open questions are tracked
