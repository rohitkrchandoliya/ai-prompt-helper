# Customer Interview Prompt Examples

## 1. Draft neutral questions
> Create interview questions to learn about [problem and audience]. Avoid leading questions, ask about past behavior before hypothetical intent, and include optional follow-ups.

## 2. Synthesize interview notes
> Analyze these anonymized notes [notes]. Group recurring themes, preserve contradictory evidence, and separate direct observations from interpretations. Do not infer demographics or motives not stated.

## 3. Turn findings into opportunities
> From these findings [evidence], suggest product opportunities and follow-up research questions. For each opportunity, cite supporting evidence and note uncertainty or missing perspectives.
