# Learning Roadmap Prompt Examples

## 1. Sequence learning topics
> Organize these topics [topics] into a learning sequence for someone at [starting level]. Explain prerequisites and dependencies, and flag where the sequence depends on prior experience.

## 2. Choose practice activities
> Suggest hands-on practice for [topic] and [goal] given [available time and tools]. Include a simple task, a stretch task, and a way to check the result.

## 3. Check mastery
> Given these practice results [results] and target skill [skill], identify what the evidence supports, what remains untested, and the next assessment that would demonstrate independent ability.
