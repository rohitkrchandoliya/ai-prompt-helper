# Business Contingency Plan Prompt Examples

A business contingency plan identifies disruptions, response priorities, communication, dependencies, and recovery decisions.

## Prompt templates

### Draft a framework
> Draft a business contingency plan for **[organization/context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Objective: **[objective]**. Use provided evidence, state assumptions, and identify missing inputs.

### Stress-test it
> Stress-test this draft against plausible edge cases, conflicting priorities, unclear ownership, and practical constraints: **[draft]**. Explain the highest-priority weaknesses and suggest mitigations.

### Finalize actions
> Produce a concise revised version and action list. Include responsible roles, dependencies, validation steps, and open questions. Do not invent unsupported details.

## Quality checklist
- Scope and intended users are clear
- Evidence and assumptions are distinct
- Edge cases and risks are considered
- Actions and ownership are explicit
- Validation is practical
