# Research Proposal Prompt Examples

## Draft a proposal
"Draft a research proposal for [question]. Include context, research gap, objectives, method, sampling, data collection, analysis, limitations, ethics considerations, timeline, and resources. Mark unsupported background claims and information requiring sources."

## Check method alignment
"Assess whether the proposed methods can answer each research question. Identify mismatches, potential bias, feasibility issues, and missing details. Recommend changes without claiming the method guarantees a result. Proposal: [paste]."

## Define limitations
"List plausible limitations for this research design and explain how each could affect interpretation. Suggest practical mitigations and language for reporting limitations transparently. Design: [paste]."
