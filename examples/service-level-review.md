# Service Level Review Prompt Examples

## Review service levels
"Compare actual service performance with the supplied service-level targets. Report calculations, period, exclusions, data gaps, and exceptions. Distinguish verified results from explanations not yet established. Data: [paste]."

## Investigate missed targets
"For each missed target, outline possible contributing factors, evidence to gather, customer impact, and immediate containment. Do not present a hypothesis as a confirmed cause. Results and context: [paste]."

## Plan service improvements
"Create an improvement plan based on the verified service-level gaps. Include action, expected effect, owner role, dependency, measure, and review date. Flag actions requiring budget, policy, or staffing approval."
