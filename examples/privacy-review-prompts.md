# Privacy Review Prompt Examples

## Review data collection
Review this feature's data collection [feature details]. Identify data categories, purpose, necessity, access, retention, and deletion expectations. Flag questions that require legal or privacy-team review.

## Improve a privacy notice
Rewrite this privacy notice excerpt [text] in plain language without changing its legal meaning. Clearly state what is collected, why, where it goes, and what choices users have; flag missing facts instead of guessing.

## Create a retention checklist
Create a data retention checklist for [data type and workflow]. Include retention rationale, access controls, deletion method, backup considerations, and verification steps. Do not assume a legal retention period without jurisdiction-specific evidence.
