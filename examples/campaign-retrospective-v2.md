# Campaign Retrospective Prompt Examples

A campaign retrospective reviews goals, outcomes, channel performance, lessons, and changes for the next cycle.

## Prompt templates

### Prepare
> Prepare a campaign retrospective for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Goal: **[goal]**. Use available evidence and explicitly note limitations.

### Diagnose
> Diagnose the strengths, gaps, risks, and potential sources of bias in this material: **[details]**. Separate facts from hypotheses and rank the issues by likely impact.

### Recommend improvements
> Recommend practical improvements with rationale, role-based ownership, dependencies, and a way to check results. Do not fabricate metrics, dates, or causes.

## Quality checklist
- Goal and audience are clear
- Evidence and uncertainty are shown
- Risks and dependencies are considered
- Recommendations are actionable
- Follow-up measures are defined
