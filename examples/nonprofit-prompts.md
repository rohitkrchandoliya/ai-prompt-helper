# Nonprofit Prompt Examples

## Draft a donation appeal
Draft a donation appeal for [cause] aimed at [audience]. Explain the verified need, how donations will be used, and how supporters can contribute. Do not invent beneficiaries, statistics, urgency, or outcomes.

## Write an impact update
Turn these verified program notes into a concise impact update: [notes]. Distinguish completed work from planned work and preserve privacy by removing identifying details.

## Recruit volunteers
Create a volunteer recruitment message for [activity]. Include tasks, time commitment, required skills, support provided, and a clear sign-up step. Avoid implying benefits or guarantees that are not confirmed.
