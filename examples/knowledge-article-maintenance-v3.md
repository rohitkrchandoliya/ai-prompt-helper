# Knowledge Article Maintenance Prompt Examples

A maintenance plan keeps knowledge articles accurate, findable, owned, and reviewed when processes change.

## Prompt templates

### Prepare a review
> Prepare a knowledge article maintenance for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Goal: **[goal]**. Use supplied information and identify evidence gaps.

### Compare and assess
> Compare the current options or materials **[details]** using relevant criteria. Explain trade-offs, uncertainty, risks, and the strongest supporting evidence.

### Recommend action
> Recommend next steps with clear rationale, role-based owners, dependencies, and observable success checks. Leave missing facts as placeholders.

## Quality checklist
- Scope and goal are explicit
- Criteria are relevant and consistent
- Evidence and uncertainty are visible
- Risks and dependencies are addressed
- Recommendations can be acted on
