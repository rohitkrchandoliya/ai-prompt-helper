# Business Process Automation Assessment Prompt Examples

An automation assessment identifies repeatable tasks, exception paths, risk, controls, and measurable benefits.

## Prompt templates

### Assess the situation
> Assess **[context/process]** for **[audience/team]**. Desired outcome: **[outcome]**. Use supplied facts, list constraints, and flag uncertainties.

### Compare approaches
> Compare these approaches: **[options]**. Evaluate value, effort, risk, dependencies, user impact, and reversibility. Explain trade-offs without making unsupported claims.

### Plan delivery
> Recommend an approach and draft a delivery plan with milestones, responsible roles, dependencies, safeguards, and completion criteria. Leave unknown details as placeholders.

## Quality checklist
- Desired outcome is measurable where possible
- Options and trade-offs are explicit
- Risks and exceptions are addressed
- Responsibilities are clear
- Review criteria are defined
