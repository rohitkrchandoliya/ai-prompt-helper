# customer churn risk review Prompt Template

Review account health signals to identify possible churn risks and retention actions. Distinguish measured behavior from assumptions, avoid treating demographic or sensitive traits as proxies, and identify missing context. Rank risks by evidence and urgency; suggest respectful outreach, value-review questions, owners, and indicators to monitor. Do not label a customer as certain to churn.

## Reusable instruction

Use only the context provided. Ask clarifying questions when missing information changes the recommendation; otherwise state assumptions. Present findings in a structured format with priorities, rationale, risks, and actionable next steps. Mark any claim that requires external verification.
