# Policy Document Review Prompt Examples

## Review policy clarity
"Review this policy for clear scope, defined terms, responsibilities, procedures, exceptions, escalation, and review cadence. Flag contradictions and ambiguous language. Do not assert legal sufficiency; identify questions for qualified reviewers. Policy: [paste]."

## Compare policy versions
"Compare these policy versions and summarize additions, deletions, changed obligations, and potential downstream impacts. Quote or reference the supplied section labels and flag changes whose meaning is unclear. Versions: [paste]."

## Create an implementation checklist
"Convert this approved policy into an implementation checklist with task, accountable role, evidence of completion, dependencies, and review date. Do not add obligations that are not supported by the policy. Policy: [paste]."
