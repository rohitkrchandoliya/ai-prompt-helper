# Survey Design Prompt Examples

## Draft neutral survey questions
"Design a short survey to learn about [research goal] from [audience]. Use neutral language, one concept per question, suitable answer options, and a sensible order. Flag sensitive questions and explain when a question may introduce bias."

## Review a questionnaire
"Audit this questionnaire for leading wording, double-barreled questions, unclear timeframes, overlapping options, missing 'not applicable' choices, and unnecessary personal data. Suggest revisions and explain each concern. Survey: [paste]."

## Plan analysis before collection
"Create an analysis plan for this survey goal. Map each research question to the items that can answer it, define how missing responses will be handled, and identify limitations. Do not imply the sample is representative unless the sampling method supports that conclusion."
