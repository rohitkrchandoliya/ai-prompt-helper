# Meeting Effectiveness Review Prompt Examples

A meeting effectiveness review checks purpose, preparation, participation, decisions, and follow-through.

## Prompt templates

### Create
> Create a meeting effectiveness review for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Goal: **[goal]**. Use supplied facts and clearly flag unknowns.

### Review
> Review this draft for clarity, completeness, consistency, and practical usability: **[draft]**. Prioritize gaps and suggest a revised version.

### Action plan
> Turn the recommendations into steps with responsible roles, dependencies, review checkpoints, and observable completion criteria. Do not invent missing details.

## Quality checklist
- Purpose and audience are clear
- Definitions are consistent
- Assumptions and limitations are visible
- Actions are practical
- Outcomes can be reviewed
