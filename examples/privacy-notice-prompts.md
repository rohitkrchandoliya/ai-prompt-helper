# Privacy Notice Prompt Examples

## 1. Simplify a notice
> Rewrite this privacy notice [text] in plain language for [audience] while preserving the stated practices, choices, and limitations. Do not add legal assurances or imply rights not in the source.

## 2. Check practice consistency
> Compare this notice [notice] with this documented data flow [flow]. Flag inconsistencies about collection, use, retention, sharing, and user controls. Mark items that need legal or privacy review.

## 3. Create a data inventory summary
> Summarize this supplied inventory [inventory] by data category, purpose, access, retention, and sharing. Identify missing fields and avoid inferring practices not documented.
