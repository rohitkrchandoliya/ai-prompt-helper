# Service Process Walkthrough Prompt Examples

A process walkthrough documents real steps, handoffs, decision points, exceptions, and friction.

## Prompt templates

### Draft
> Draft a service process walkthrough for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Objective: **[objective]**. Use supplied facts and clearly note missing information.

### Review
> Review this draft **[material]** for clarity, coverage, consistency, usability, and risks. Rank gaps and suggest specific improvements.

### Apply
> Turn the recommendations into a practical checklist with responsible roles, dependencies, validation steps, and follow-up questions.

## Quality checklist
- Audience and purpose are clear
- Evidence and assumptions are distinguished
- Risks and edge cases are covered
- Actions are specific
- Completion can be verified
