# Travel Planning Prompt Examples

## Plan an itinerary
Plan a [duration]-day trip to [destination] for [travelers]. Respect these interests, mobility needs, and constraints: [details]. Group nearby activities, include rest time, and flag opening hours or transport details that need current verification.

## Build a packing checklist
Create a packing list for [destination] during [season] for [activities]. Separate essentials from optional items and account for baggage limits and any special requirements I provide.

## Estimate a trip budget
Estimate a budget for [trip details]. Show transport, lodging, food, local transit, activities, and contingency separately. Label assumptions and do not present estimates as live prices.
