# Webinar Follow-Up Sequence Prompt Examples

A webinar follow-up sequence segments attendees and non-attendees, provides relevant resources, and uses a clear next step.

## Prompt templates

### Draft
> Draft a webinar follow-up sequence for **[organization/context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Goal: **[goal]**. Use supplied facts only, note uncertainties, and state where specialist review is required.

### Validate
> Check this draft for completeness, consistency, feasibility, and unsupported statements: **[draft]**. List issues by severity and provide specific corrections.

### Operationalize
> Convert the approved direction into a practical workflow for **[team]**. Include steps, responsible roles, inputs, outputs, exceptions, and review points. Leave unknown details as placeholders.

## Quality checklist
- Audience and purpose are clear
- Assumptions and limitations are stated
- Steps or measures are specific
- Risks and exceptions are considered
- Review responsibilities are defined
