# Research Interview Synthesis Prompt Examples

## Synthesize notes
"Synthesize these interview notes into themes, supporting excerpts, exceptions, and open questions. Keep participant evidence separate from interpretation and avoid implying frequency beyond the sample. Notes: [paste]."

## Compare participant perspectives
"Compare the supplied interviews by needs, behaviors, barriers, and workarounds. Highlight meaningful differences and contradictory evidence. Use anonymous participant labels and do not infer sensitive attributes. Interviews: [paste]."

## Turn findings into opportunities
"Translate the supported findings into opportunity statements and follow-up research questions. Include the evidence behind each opportunity, uncertainty, and a method to validate it before prioritizing solutions. Findings: [paste]."
