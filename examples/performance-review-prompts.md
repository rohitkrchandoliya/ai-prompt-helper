# Performance Review Prompt Examples

## 1. Draft a self-review
> Use these notes and outcomes [evidence] to draft a balanced self-review. Connect achievements to impact, acknowledge learning areas, and do not invent metrics or accomplishments.

## 2. Set measurable goals
> Turn these priorities [priorities] into clear goals with success measures, milestones, dependencies, and review checkpoints. Flag targets that need agreement or baseline data.

## 3. Make feedback constructive
> Rewrite this feedback [text] to be specific, respectful, behavior-focused, and actionable. Preserve the core concern and avoid assumptions about intent or personality.
