# Job Search Prompt Examples

## Assess job fit
Compare this job posting [posting] with my actual background [background]. Summarize matching requirements, gaps, location and work-authorization constraints, and questions to verify with the employer.

## Organize applications
Create a job-application tracker structure for [roles and timeframe]. Include employer, role, location, deadline, required documents, visa/relocation details to verify, status, and next action.

## Draft recruiter outreach
Draft a concise message to [recruiter or employer] about [role]. Use only my verified experience, ask a specific relevant question, and avoid overstating eligibility or interest.
