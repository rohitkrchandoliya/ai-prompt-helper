# Research Findings Brief Prompt Examples

A research findings brief presents the question, method, evidence, limitations, and practical implications.

## Prompt templates

### Prepare a brief
> Prepare a research findings brief for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Purpose: **[purpose]**. Use supplied evidence and make limitations explicit.

### Analyze material
> Analyze this material **[material]** for patterns, anomalies, unresolved questions, and alternative explanations. Separate observed facts from interpretations.

### Recommend next steps
> Recommend practical next steps with rationale, responsible roles, dependencies, and criteria for checking progress. Do not invent facts or benchmarks.

## Quality checklist
- Question and audience are clear
- Evidence and interpretations are separated
- Limitations are stated
- Recommendations follow from findings
- Next steps are verifiable
