# Employee Recognition Program Prompt Examples

An employee recognition program defines fair criteria, inclusive practices, meaningful recognition, and evaluation.

## Prompt templates

### Create
> Create a employee recognition program for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Objective: **[objective]**. Base conclusions on supplied evidence and identify missing inputs.

### Evaluate
> Evaluate the following draft or situation: **[details]**. Assess clarity, completeness, assumptions, practical constraints, and potential unintended effects. Prioritize recommendations and explain their rationale.

### Implement
> Turn the agreed recommendations into a plan for **[team/context]**. List work items, dependencies, responsible roles, measures of success, and review checkpoints. Use placeholders for unknown owners or dates.

## Quality checklist
- Objective, audience, and scope are clear
- Evidence and assumptions are distinguished
- Constraints and risks are acknowledged
- Recommendations are prioritized
- Success measures are observable
