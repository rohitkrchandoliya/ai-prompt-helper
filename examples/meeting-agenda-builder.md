# Meeting Agenda Builder Prompt Examples

Prompts for focused meetings with clear outcomes.

## Build an outcome-based agenda
"Create a [duration]-minute agenda for [meeting purpose]. For each item include the desired outcome, owner, time allocation, and preparation needed. Reserve time for decisions and next steps. Participants: [roles]."

## Reduce meeting scope
"Review this draft agenda and identify items that can be handled asynchronously, combined, or removed. Keep the meeting focused on decisions and discussion that benefit from real-time collaboration. Draft agenda: [paste]."

## Facilitate a decision
"Design a meeting plan to decide [decision]. Include the decision criteria, options to compare, key risks, dissenting views to invite, decision owner, and a closing recap of the choice and action items."
