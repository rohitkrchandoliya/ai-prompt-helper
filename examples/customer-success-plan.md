# Customer Success Plan Prompt Examples

## Build a success plan
"Create a customer success plan for [customer segment] using the goals and context supplied. Include desired outcomes, adoption milestones, enablement, success measures, owners, risks, and review cadence. Mark unknowns instead of inventing account details."

## Prepare a check-in
"Draft a customer check-in agenda covering progress toward agreed goals, product adoption, blockers, feedback, upcoming milestones, and actions. Keep questions collaborative and do not assume the customer is satisfied or dissatisfied."

## Review account health
"Assess the supplied account notes against transparent health indicators such as adoption, outcome progress, support friction, engagement, and renewal timeline. Explain evidence for each rating and identify missing data. Notes: [paste]."
