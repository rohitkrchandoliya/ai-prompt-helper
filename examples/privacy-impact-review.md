# Privacy Impact Review Prompt Examples

## Map data handling
"Create a preliminary privacy review for [product or process]. Map personal data categories, purpose, collection points, recipients, retention, access controls, and deletion practices based only on supplied facts. Identify unknowns for the privacy or legal team."

## Assess proposed changes
"Review this proposed feature for privacy risks including excessive collection, unexpected reuse, access, retention, disclosure, user control, and security. For each risk, describe affected people, likely impact, mitigation options, and questions needing expert review. Feature: [paste]."

## Write a privacy checklist
"Create a launch checklist for privacy review with evidence required for each item, accountable role, completion status, and unresolved decision. Do not claim legal compliance; identify jurisdictions and applicable requirements that need confirmation."
