# Customer Journey Metrics Prompt Examples

Customer journey metrics connect stages of an experience to user goals, measurable signals, and data limitations.

## Prompt templates

### Create
> Create a customer journey metrics for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Desired outcome: **[outcome]**. Use supplied information, flag unknowns, and make the output easy to apply.

### Improve
> Review this draft for clarity, completeness, evidence, and feasibility: **[draft]**. Identify the most important gaps and return a revised version with assumptions labeled.

### Action plan
> Turn the recommendations into a practical sequence. Include responsible roles, dependencies, review checkpoints, and observable completion criteria. Do not invent missing dates or metrics.

## Quality checklist
- Goal and audience are stated
- Assumptions are separated from facts
- Risks and dependencies are included
- Actions are specific and verifiable
- Open questions are recorded
