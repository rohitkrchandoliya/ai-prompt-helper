# Team Retrospective Prompt Examples

## 1. Structure a retrospective
> Create a retrospective agenda for [team and period] with prompts about what helped, what hindered, and what to try next. Encourage specific examples and a blameless discussion.

## 2. Summarize themes
> Synthesize these anonymized retrospective notes [notes]. Group repeated themes, preserve minority views, and avoid attributing comments to individuals unless explicitly needed and authorized.

## 3. Create follow-up actions
> Turn these themes [themes] into a short list of experiments or actions with an owner role, success signal, and review point. Prioritize realistic changes over vague commitments.
