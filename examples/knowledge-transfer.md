# Knowledge Transfer Plan Prompt Examples

## Plan a handover
"Create a knowledge-transfer plan for [role, system, or project]. Include critical knowledge areas, source materials, walkthroughs, practice tasks, receiving owner, verification method, and target dates. Highlight single points of failure and unknowns."

## Structure a handover session
"Build a [duration]-minute handover agenda covering system context, recurring tasks, exceptions, access ownership, unresolved issues, and escalation paths. Include time for the recipient to demonstrate understanding and ask questions."

## Verify readiness
"Create a handover-readiness checklist that tests whether the receiving person can independently complete key tasks, locate documentation, handle common failures, and identify escalation contacts. Do not equate document delivery with successful transfer."
