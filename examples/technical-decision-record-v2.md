# Technical Decision Record Prompt Examples

A technical decision record documents context, considered options, trade-offs, and the rationale for a choice.

## Prompt templates

### Draft
> Draft a technical decision record for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Desired result: **[result]**. Base statements on supplied material and flag unknowns.

### Analyze
> Analyze this information: **[material]**. Extract key facts, patterns, alternative explanations, risks, and unresolved questions. Separate observations from conclusions.

### Finalize
> Produce a concise final artifact from the analysis. Include the rationale, trade-offs, responsibilities or next steps, and a review point where relevant. Do not invent details.

## Quality checklist
- Context and objective are stated
- Evidence and interpretations are distinct
- Alternatives and trade-offs are considered
- Risks and open questions are recorded
- Next steps or decision rationale are clear
