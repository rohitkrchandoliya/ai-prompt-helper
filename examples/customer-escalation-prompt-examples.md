# Customer Escalation Playbook Prompt Examples

A customer escalation playbook clarifies severity, communication cadence, ownership, and resolution follow-through.

## Prompt templates

### Draft
> Create a customer escalation playbook for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Objective: **[objective]**. Base the response on provided facts, clearly mark assumptions, and list missing inputs.

### Check quality
> Check this draft for clarity, completeness, evidence, feasibility, and risk: **[draft]**. Prioritize the most important improvements and rewrite the sections that need work.

### Plan next steps
> Based on these findings **[findings]**, propose a staged plan with actions, responsible roles, dependencies, and observable success criteria. Do not fabricate names, dates, or numbers.

## Checklist
- Goal and audience are stated
- Evidence supports conclusions
- Assumptions are labeled
- Risks and dependencies are considered
- Next steps are concrete and verifiable
