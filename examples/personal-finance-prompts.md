# Personal Finance Education Prompt Examples

## Build a basic budget
Help organize these monthly income and expense figures: [figures]. Categorize needs, wants, savings, and irregular costs; state assumptions and flag missing information.

## Compare a savings goal
Help me plan toward a savings target of [amount] by [date]. Show the arithmetic, possible monthly contributions, and trade-offs. Do not assume an interest rate unless I provide one.

## Compare purchase costs
Compare the total cost of [options] using purchase price, recurring costs, maintenance, and expected lifespan. Separate known figures from estimates and avoid claiming this is individualized financial advice.
