# Product Feedback Interview Prompt Examples

## Prepare feedback questions
"Create neutral interview questions to understand how users experienced [feature]. Ask about the last real use, expected outcome, friction, workarounds, and perceived value. Avoid leading questions and do not pitch a solution."

## Probe usability friction
"Suggest follow-up questions for this user comment: [comment]. Explore the situation, frequency, consequences, and expectations without assuming the cause. Keep the questions neutral and concise."

## Synthesize findings
"Organize these feedback notes into user goals, observed friction, direct evidence, severity as described by participants, and follow-up questions. Separate interpretation from quotes and avoid generalizing beyond the sample. Notes: [paste]."
