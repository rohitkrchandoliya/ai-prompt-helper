# Team Learning Retrospective Prompt Examples

A learning retrospective turns experience into observations, lessons, and specific experiments for the next cycle.

## Prompt templates

### Prepare
> Prepare a team learning retrospective for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Goal: **[goal]**. Use confirmed facts and flag unknowns.

### Review
> Review the supplied material **[material]** for clarity, completeness, recurring issues, and practical usability. Identify priority gaps and propose revisions.

### Next steps
> Create a short action plan with role-based owners, dependencies, a way to verify completion, and a date placeholder for review.

## Quality checklist
- Goal and scope are explicit
- Evidence and assumptions are separated
- Issues are prioritized with rationale
- Actions are practical
- Follow-up can be verified
