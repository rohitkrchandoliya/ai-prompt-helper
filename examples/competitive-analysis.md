# Competitive Analysis Prompt Examples

## Structure a competitor review
"Create a competitor-analysis framework for [market and audience]. Compare offerings, target users, positioning, pricing model, distribution, strengths, limitations, and evidence quality. Separate sourced facts from hypotheses and identify gaps requiring research."

## Compare alternatives
"Compare these alternatives against the decision criteria supplied. Use a table, cite the provided evidence by source label, flag incomparable data, and avoid treating missing information as a negative finding. Alternatives and evidence: [paste]."

## Identify strategic questions
"Based on this competitor review, identify the most important unanswered questions, potential differentiation opportunities, and risks of overinterpreting limited evidence. Do not claim market leadership or market share without supporting data. Review: [paste]."
