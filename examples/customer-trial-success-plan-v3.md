# Customer Trial Success Plan Prompt Examples

A trial success plan defines what users should try, what evidence indicates value, and what support is needed.

## Prompt templates

### Create
> Create a customer trial success plan for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Goal: **[goal]**. Use supplied facts and list missing inputs.

### Review
> Review this draft for accuracy, clarity, completeness, and ease of use: **[draft]**. Identify gaps and recommend specific revisions.

### Apply
> Convert the approved draft into steps with role-based responsibilities, dependencies, exceptions, and observable completion criteria.

## Quality checklist
- Goal and audience are clear
- Information is accurate and qualified
- Responsibilities are explicit
- Exceptions are covered
- Next steps are verifiable
