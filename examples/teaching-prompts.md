# Teaching Prompt Examples

## Plan a lesson
Plan a [duration]-minute lesson on [topic] for learners at [level]. Include measurable objectives, materials, timed activities, a quick check for understanding, and an accessible alternative.

## Differentiate an activity
Adapt this activity for learners with different prior knowledge and learning needs: [activity]. Keep the core objective consistent and offer options without stereotyping learners.

## Check understanding
Create five short questions about [topic], moving from recall to application. Provide an answer key and explain what common incorrect answers may reveal.
