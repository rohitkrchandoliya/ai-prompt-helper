# On-Call Runbook Prompt Examples

## Draft a runbook
"Draft an on-call runbook for [service or alert]. Include symptoms, severity guidance, initial checks, safe diagnostic commands from supplied documentation, escalation criteria, communication steps, and recovery verification. Flag missing permissions or commands for owner review."

## Improve an alert
"Review this alert definition and its runbook for unclear thresholds, noisy conditions, missing context, and unsafe remediation. Recommend changes and identify the evidence needed to validate them. Alert and runbook: [paste]."

## Document an incident handoff
"Create a concise shift handoff with current impact, confirmed facts, actions taken, outstanding hypotheses, next steps, owner, and escalation path. Do not mark an issue resolved unless recovery is verified. Notes: [paste]."
