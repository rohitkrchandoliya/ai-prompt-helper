# Marketing Prompt Examples

## Define an audience
Describe potential audience segments for [offer] in [market]. For each, list needs, likely objections, and ways to validate the assumptions. Do not invent demographic research or claim certainty without evidence.

## Build a content plan
Create a [duration]-week content plan for [channel] with [goal]. Include topic, audience need, format, call to action, and a simple measurement for each item. Keep workload within [capacity].

## Review marketing copy
Review this copy for clarity, relevance, accessibility, unsupported claims, and fit with the intended audience: [copy]. Suggest precise edits and flag claims that require substantiation.
