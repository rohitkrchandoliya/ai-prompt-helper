# Research Paper Prompt Examples

## 1. Summarize a paper
> Summarize this paper or excerpt [text] with research question, methods, findings, and stated limitations. Clearly distinguish the authors' claims from your interpretation and note information absent from the excerpt.

## 2. Evaluate methodology
> Review the described methodology [text] for sample selection, measurement, confounders, uncertainty, and generalizability. Do not infer flaws without evidence; list questions a reader should verify.

## 3. Build an evidence table
> Extract the paper's research question, sample, method, key result, limitations, and relevance to [topic] into a table. Use only supplied text and mark unavailable details as not stated.
