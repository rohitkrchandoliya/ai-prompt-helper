# Creative Prompt Examples

## Generate distinct concepts
Generate [number] distinct concepts for [project]. Give each a different central idea, audience appeal, and practical constraint. Avoid superficial variations of the same concept.

## Develop a story premise
Help develop a story about [premise] in [genre]. Suggest a protagonist goal, opposing force, stakes, and a meaningful choice. Ask before deciding details I have not specified.

## Critique a draft
Give constructive feedback on this creative draft: [draft]. Discuss clarity, pacing, originality, and emotional effect, then suggest a few targeted revisions while preserving my intent.
