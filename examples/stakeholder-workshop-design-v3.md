# Stakeholder Workshop Design Prompt Examples

A stakeholder workshop design aligns participants, decisions, preparation, facilitation, and follow-up.

## Prompt templates

### Plan
> Create a stakeholder workshop design for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Goal: **[goal]**. Use confirmed information, mark assumptions, and list missing inputs.

### Review
> Review this draft or source material: **[material]**. Identify gaps, conflicting information, risks, and areas where evidence is weak. Suggest prioritized improvements.

### Execute
> Turn the recommendations into an actionable sequence with responsible roles, dependencies, communication needs, and verification criteria. Leave unknown values as placeholders.

## Quality checklist
- Goal and scope are clear
- Facts and assumptions are distinguished
- Risks and dependencies are addressed
- Actions have clear ownership
- Completion can be verified
