# Podcast Planning Prompt Examples

## 1. Outline an episode
> Create an episode outline for [topic, audience, duration]. Include an opening, key segments, transitions, and closing. Mark factual claims that need verification and do not invent guest details.

## 2. Prepare interview questions
> Draft open-ended interview questions for [guest role and topic]. Avoid leading questions, group them by theme, and include optional follow-ups that invite concrete examples.

## 3. Create show notes
> Turn this transcript or outline [source] into show notes with a concise summary, key topics, and timestamps only when supplied. Do not invent quotes or links.
