# Remote Meeting Facilitation Prompt Examples

Remote facilitation needs clear outcomes, inclusive participation, timeboxes, and explicit decisions.

## Prompt templates

### 1. Create a first draft
> Act as a skilled practitioner. Prepare a remote meeting facilitation for **[context]**. The intended audience is **[audience]** and the desired outcome is **[outcome]**. Use the supplied facts only; flag missing information instead of guessing. Organize the output into a clear sequence with actionable recommendations.

### 2. Critique and refine
> Review this draft: **[paste draft]**. Identify ambiguity, missing context, unsupported claims, and practical risks. Rank improvements by impact, then provide a revised version while preserving verified details.

### 3. Adapt the approach
> Tailor the material for **[audience/use case]**, using a **[tone]** tone and **[length]** level of detail. Explain any trade-offs and list questions that must be resolved before implementation. Source material: **[paste material]**.

## Quality checklist
- Objective and audience are explicit.
- Facts and assumptions are distinguished.
- Recommendations are practical and specific.
- Risks, dependencies, and trade-offs are visible.
- Owners or next steps are clear where applicable.
