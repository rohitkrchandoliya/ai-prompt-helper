# Customer Communications Calendar Prompt Examples

A communications calendar coordinates audience, purpose, channel, timing, dependencies, and review.

## Prompt templates

### Create
> Create a customer communications calendar for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Purpose: **[purpose]**. Use known details and mark missing information.

### Review
> Review the draft **[draft]** for consistency, completeness, feasibility, and clarity. Identify ambiguities, risks, dependencies, and improvements.

### Operationalize
> Turn the final approach into a checklist with responsibilities, review points, validation steps, and criteria for completion.

## Quality checklist
- Scope and terms are clear
- Assumptions are labeled
- Risks and dependencies are documented
- Responsibilities are explicit
- Validation criteria are usable
