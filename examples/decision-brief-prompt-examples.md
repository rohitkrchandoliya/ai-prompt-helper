# Decision Brief Prompt Examples

A decision brief helps a reader choose among options using clear criteria, evidence, trade-offs, and a recommendation.

## Prompt templates

### 1. Draft a first version
> Act as an experienced practitioner. Create a decision brief for **[project/context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Goal: **[goal]**. Use only the information provided; mark unknowns as questions rather than inventing facts. Structure the result with a concise overview, key details, assumptions, risks, and next steps where relevant.

### 2. Review and improve
> Review this draft for clarity, completeness, consistency, and usefulness: **[paste draft]**. Identify the highest-impact gaps, explain why they matter, and provide a revised version. Preserve verified facts and label any assumptions.

### 3. Tailor to a specific audience
> Adapt the following decision brief for **[audience]** with **[tone/level of detail]**. Keep the objective unchanged, define specialist terms, and separate evidence from recommendations. Draft: **[paste material]**.

## Quality checklist
- State the objective and intended audience.
- Separate confirmed facts, assumptions, and open questions.
- Use specific, actionable language.
- Highlight material risks, dependencies, and trade-offs.
- End with clear next steps or acceptance criteria.
