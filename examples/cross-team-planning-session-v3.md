# Cross-Team Planning Session Prompt Examples

A cross-team planning session aligns shared outcomes, dependencies, decisions, and next actions across groups.

## Prompt templates

### Prepare
> Prepare a cross-team planning session for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Objective: **[objective]**. Use supplied information and flag missing inputs.

### Facilitate review
> Review these notes or materials **[material]**. Identify shared priorities, conflicts, unclear responsibilities, dependencies, and open questions. Distinguish facts from assumptions.

### Confirm actions
> Produce a concise action list with role-based owners, dependencies, decision points, and a method to confirm completion.

## Quality checklist
- Objective and participants are clear
- Evidence and assumptions are separated
- Dependencies and conflicts are visible
- Decisions and owners are documented
- Follow-up is actionable
