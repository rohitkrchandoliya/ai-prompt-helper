# Product Analytics Prompt Examples

## 1. Define a metric
> Propose a precise definition for [metric] including numerator, denominator, event conditions, time window, exclusions, and known limitations. Ask for clarification where product behavior is unspecified.

## 2. Analyze a funnel
> Analyze these funnel counts [data]. Calculate step-to-step conversion where possible, call out missing denominators and sample limits, and distinguish observed patterns from causal explanations.

## 3. Summarize a product experiment
> Summarize these experiment results [results and method]. Include primary metric, uncertainty, guardrails, and limitations. Do not claim causation or statistical significance unless supported by the supplied analysis.
