# Training Needs Analysis Prompt Examples

## Identify learning needs
"Create a training-needs analysis for [role or team] expected to perform [tasks]. Separate skill gaps supported by evidence from assumptions. Include desired performance, current evidence, likely barriers, and questions for learners and managers."

## Prioritize training topics
"Prioritize these proposed training topics using business impact, frequency of task, risk of errors, learner need, and feasibility. Explain the criteria and identify issues that may require process or tool changes rather than training alone. Topics: [paste]."

## Evaluate learning outcomes
"Propose measurable learning outcomes and an evaluation plan for [training]. Include a baseline, practice task, success criteria, learner feedback, and a follow-up check for on-the-job application. Avoid treating attendance as proof of competence."
