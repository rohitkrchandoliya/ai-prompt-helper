# Annual Planning Prompt Examples

## Build an annual plan
"Create an annual planning outline for [team or organization] using the stated strategy, resources, and constraints. Include objectives, initiatives, milestones, measures, owners by role, dependencies, risks, and review cadence. Flag assumptions and missing decisions."

## Align goals and work
"Map each proposed initiative to the stated objectives and explain its expected contribution, resource need, evidence, and trade-offs. Identify work that is weakly aligned or duplicates another effort. Initiatives: [paste]."

## Create quarterly checkpoints
"Break the annual plan into quarterly checkpoints with measurable outcomes, leading indicators, decision points, and conditions that may require reprioritization. Avoid inventing numeric targets where no baseline is supplied."
