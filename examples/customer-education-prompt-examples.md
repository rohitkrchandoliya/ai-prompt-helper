# Customer Education Program Prompt Examples

A customer education program defines learning goals, formats, sequencing, accessibility, and measures of usefulness.

## Prompt templates

### Create a plan
> Create a customer education program for **[organization/context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Goal: **[goal]**. Use supplied information, flag uncertainties, and identify any specialist review needed.

### Identify gaps
> Assess this draft or current process: **[details]**. Identify omissions, unclear responsibilities, inconsistent practices, and risks. Rank the issues and suggest practical fixes.

### Define follow-through
> Convert the agreed fixes into a plan with action, rationale, responsible role, dependency, due-date placeholder, and evidence of completion. Do not invent missing facts.

## Quality checklist
- Purpose and scope are clear
- Claims are supported or qualified
- Responsibilities are explicit
- Risks and dependencies are noted
- Follow-up can be verified
