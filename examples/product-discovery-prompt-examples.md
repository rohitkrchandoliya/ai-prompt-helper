# Product Discovery Workshop Prompt Examples

Product discovery workshops align teams on user problems, assumptions, evidence, and experiments.

## Prompt templates

### Prepare
> Prepare a product discovery workshop for **[team/context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Goal: **[goal]**. Use supplied evidence, label assumptions, and state the important open questions.

### Analyze
> Analyze this material: **[paste details]**. Find recurring themes, contradictions, gaps, and possible bias. Distinguish observations from interpretations and rank findings by likely importance.

### Turn insights into action
> Convert the findings into a prioritized action plan. Include proposed actions, rationale, responsible roles, dependencies, and how outcomes will be checked. Do not invent metrics or deadlines.

## Quality checklist
- Purpose and users are defined
- Terms and measures are unambiguous
- Evidence and interpretation are separated
- Limitations and risks are noted
- Follow-up actions are testable
