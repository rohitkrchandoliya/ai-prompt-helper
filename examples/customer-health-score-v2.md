# Customer Health Score Design Prompt Examples

A customer health score defines transparent indicators, weighting assumptions, data sources, and appropriate interpretation.

## Prompt templates

### Draft
> Create a customer health score design for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Goal: **[goal]**. Use supplied information, flag unknowns, and structure the output into actionable sections.

### Review
> Review this draft for clarity, completeness, feasibility, and unsupported assumptions: **[draft]**. Prioritize gaps and return an improved version.

### Execution plan
> Turn the approved approach into steps with responsible roles, dependencies, verification methods, and open questions. Do not invent dates, names, or metrics.

## Quality checklist
- Purpose and audience are explicit
- Facts and assumptions are distinguished
- Risks and dependencies are included
- Recommendations are actionable
- Completion criteria are verifiable
