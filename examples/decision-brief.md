# Decision Brief Prompt Examples

## Prepare a decision brief
"Prepare a one-page decision brief for [decision]. Include the decision required, deadline, context, options, agreed criteria, evidence, trade-offs, risks, recommendation if supported, and unresolved questions. Distinguish facts from assumptions."

## Compare options fairly
"Compare these options using the supplied criteria. Show evidence for each assessment, identify missing data, and explain sensitivity to uncertain assumptions. Do not force a winner if the evidence is insufficient. Options and criteria: [paste]."

## Capture the decision
"Create a decision record with the choice, rationale, alternatives considered, dissenting concerns, assumptions, owner, date, and conditions that would trigger reconsideration. Use placeholders for any details not provided."
