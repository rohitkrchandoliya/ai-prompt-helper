# Presentation Prompt Examples

## Outline a presentation
Create a [length]-slide outline about [topic] for [audience] with the goal of [goal]. Give each slide one main point, supporting evidence, and a logical transition.

## Write speaker notes
Write natural speaker notes for these slides: [outline]. Expand on the slide rather than reading bullets aloud, and mark where evidence or a visual is needed.

## Simplify dense slides
Review this slide content: [content]. Identify the core message, suggest what to remove or move to notes, and propose a clear title that states the takeaway.
