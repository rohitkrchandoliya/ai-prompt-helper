# Service Change Notice Prompt Examples

A service change notice explains what changes, who is affected, when it happens, and where to get help.

## Prompt templates

### Prepare
> Prepare a service change notice for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Goal: **[goal]**. Base the output on confirmed details and identify missing information.

### Check quality
> Review the draft for clarity, accuracy, relevance, and completeness: **[draft]**. Identify unsupported statements and suggest specific edits.

### Follow through
> List practical next steps, responsible roles, dependencies, questions to resolve, and checks that confirm the intended outcome.

## Quality checklist
- Audience and goal are clear
- Facts and assumptions are distinguished
- Language is accessible
- Responsibilities are explicit
- Follow-up is actionable
