# Service Improvement Experiment Prompt Examples

A service improvement experiment tests a focused change, defines what success would look like, and captures lessons without overstating results.

## Prompt templates

### Define the experiment
> Propose a small experiment to improve **[service/process]** for **[audience]**. State the problem, hypothesis, scope, constraints, and evidence needed.

### Plan measurement
> Suggest appropriate outcome and guardrail measures for this experiment. Explain data limitations and avoid inventing baseline values or success thresholds.

### Review results
> Review these results **[results]** against the stated hypothesis. Separate observations from interpretations, note limitations, and recommend whether to adapt, repeat, or stop the experiment.

## Quality checklist
- The problem and hypothesis are explicit
- Scope and safeguards are appropriate
- Measures are defined before the test
- Limitations are acknowledged
- The decision and next steps are documented
