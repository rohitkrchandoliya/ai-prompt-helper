# Service Desk Knowledge Article Prompt Examples

A service desk knowledge article helps agents resolve common issues consistently and safely.

## Prompt templates

### Draft
> Draft a service desk knowledge article for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Objective: **[objective]**. Use supplied facts and clearly identify unknowns.

### Review
> Review this draft for accuracy, clarity, completeness, and audience fit: **[draft]**. List important gaps and provide a revised version.

### Apply
> Convert the approved material into a practical checklist with role-based responsibilities, dependencies, validation steps, and questions requiring follow-up.

## Quality checklist
- Audience and objective are explicit
- Claims are evidence-based
- Uncertainty is visible
- Steps are practical and accessible
- Follow-up criteria are clear
