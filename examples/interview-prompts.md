# Interview Preparation Prompt Examples

## Practice a role interview
Act as an interviewer for [role] at [seniority]. Ask one question at a time, wait for my answer, then give specific feedback on relevance, clarity, evidence, and structure. Do not assume experience I have not shared.

## Structure an example answer
Help me structure an answer about [real experience] using situation, task, action, and result. Ask for missing details instead of inventing metrics or outcomes.

## Compare role requirements
Compare this job description with my actual experience: [job description] and [experience]. Identify supported matches, gaps, and honest ways to address gaps. Do not fabricate qualifications.
