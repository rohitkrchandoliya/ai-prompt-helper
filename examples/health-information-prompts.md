# General Health Information Prompt Examples

## Explain a health term
Explain [term or test name] in plain language. Distinguish general information from interpretation of an individual case, explain common limitations, and suggest questions for a qualified healthcare professional.

## Prepare an appointment
Help me prepare questions for a healthcare appointment about [topic]. Organize symptoms or concerns I provide, questions to ask, and information I may want to bring. Do not diagnose me.

## Compare general guidance
Summarize general guidance about [wellness topic] from reputable health organizations. Note where recommendations vary, what needs professional advice, and any uncertainty; do not recommend changing prescribed treatment.
