# Partnership Proposal Prompt Examples

## Draft a proposal outline
"Create a partnership proposal outline for [organizations] focused on [shared goal]. Include mutual value, audience fit, proposed activities, responsibilities, resource needs, success measures, risks, timeline, and open questions. Do not assume either party has agreed."

## Compare partnership models
"Compare these possible partnership models by shared value, effort, governance, cost, reputation considerations, operational dependencies, and reversibility. State which assumptions need validation before choosing. Models: [paste]."

## Prepare a discussion brief
"Prepare a meeting brief for exploring a partnership. Include common ground, questions about objectives and capacity, possible pilot scope, decision process, and next steps. Avoid implying commitments that have not been made."
