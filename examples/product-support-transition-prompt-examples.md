# Product Support Transition Plan Prompt Examples

A product support transition plan transfers context, ownership, escalation paths, and operational readiness.

## Prompt templates

### Draft
> Prepare a product support transition plan for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Objective: **[objective]**. Use the information provided and mark uncertainties clearly.

### Review
> Review this draft for clarity, completeness, evidence, ownership, and practical feasibility: **[draft]**. List the most important gaps and provide an improved version.

### Next steps
> Create a prioritized action list from these findings: **[findings]**. Include responsible roles, dependencies, evidence of completion, and unresolved questions.

## Checklist
- Scope and expected outcome are explicit
- Evidence is traceable and claims are qualified
- Responsibilities and dependencies are clear
- Risks and open questions are visible
- Completion criteria are actionable
