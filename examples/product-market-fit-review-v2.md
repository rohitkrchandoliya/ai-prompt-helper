# Product-Market Fit Review Prompt Examples

A product-market fit review evaluates target-user evidence, repeated usage, willingness to pay, retention, and uncertainty.

## Prompt templates

### Create
> Create a product-market fit review for **[team/product/context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Desired outcome: **[outcome]**. Use supplied evidence and identify unknowns.

### Evaluate
> Evaluate the available evidence **[details]** against the stated goal. Distinguish observations from assumptions, explain limitations, and list the strongest unanswered questions.

### Next steps
> Recommend the next practical steps, including role-based owners, dependencies, learning or verification activities, and a checkpoint for reassessment.

## Quality checklist
- Goal and audience are defined
- Evidence is clearly separated from hypotheses
- Limitations and open questions are explicit
- Actions are practical and assigned by role
- Reassessment criteria are clear
