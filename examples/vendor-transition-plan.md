# Vendor Transition Plan Prompt Examples

## Plan a vendor transition
"Create a transition plan from [current vendor or process] to [target]. Include scope, data and asset transfer, access changes, dependencies, testing, communications, continuity, rollback, and accountable roles. Flag contractual and security questions for qualified reviewers."

## Assess transition risks
"Identify risks in this transition, including service interruption, data loss, access gaps, undocumented dependencies, cost changes, and exit constraints. For each, provide a validation step, mitigation, fallback, and owner role."

## Confirm readiness
"Create a go/no-go checklist for the transition with evidence required, responsible owner, completion status, unresolved blockers, rollback trigger, and post-transition validation. Do not treat planned tasks as completed."
