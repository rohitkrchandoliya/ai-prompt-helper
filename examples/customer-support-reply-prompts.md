# Customer Support Reply Prompt Examples

## 1. Draft a support reply
> Using this customer message [message] and verified policy [policy], draft a clear, empathetic reply. Do not promise refunds, timelines, or exceptions not authorized by the policy.

## 2. Ask for clarification
> Identify the minimum information needed to resolve this issue [issue]. Draft concise questions and remind the customer not to share passwords or unnecessary sensitive data.

## 3. Prepare an escalation
> Summarize this support case [case] for the next team with issue, steps tried, evidence, customer impact, and requested action. Exclude irrelevant personal data and distinguish confirmed facts from guesses.
