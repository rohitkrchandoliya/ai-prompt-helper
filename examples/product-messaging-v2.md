# Product Messaging Framework Prompt Examples

A product messaging framework clarifies audience, problem, value proposition, proof, and differentiators.

## Prompt templates

### Prepare a draft
> Prepare a product messaging framework for **[organization/context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Desired outcome: **[outcome]**. Base recommendations on supplied facts and label missing information.

### Assess options
> Assess these options or current findings: **[details]**. Compare likely impact, effort, dependencies, risks, and uncertainty. Explain the reasoning and avoid unsupported precision.

### Turn findings into action
> Convert the preferred approach into an action plan with work items, responsible roles, dependencies, verification steps, and open questions.

## Checklist
- Goal and scope are clear
- Assumptions are visible
- Trade-offs and risks are discussed
- Recommendations are practical
- Outcomes can be reviewed
