# Internal Communications Plan Prompt Examples

## Build a communications plan
"Create an internal communications plan for [initiative]. Map audience, purpose, key message, channel, timing, accountable role, feedback mechanism, and success signal. Identify audiences who may need additional context or accessible formats."

## Prepare a change announcement
"Draft a clear announcement about [change] using only confirmed details. Explain what is changing, why, when, who is affected, what people need to do, where to ask questions, and what remains undecided."

## Check message alignment
"Compare these communications for consistent facts, dates, terminology, and calls to action. Flag contradictions and unsupported promises. Suggest corrections while preserving audience-specific detail. Materials: [paste]."
