# General Legal Information Prompt Examples

## Explain a legal term
Explain [term] in plain language for [jurisdiction, if known]. State that laws vary by location, identify facts that could change the explanation, and suggest questions for a qualified lawyer.

## Organize a document review
Help summarize this document [non-sensitive excerpt]. Identify key dates, obligations, unclear terms, and questions to ask a legal professional. Do not claim to provide a binding legal interpretation.

## Prepare for a consultation
Create a checklist of facts and questions for a consultation about [issue]. Separate documents I already have from information I still need to gather.
