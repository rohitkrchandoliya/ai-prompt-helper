# Research Protocol Outline Prompt Examples

A research protocol makes the question, method, sample, measures, and limitations explicit before work begins.

## Prompt templates

### Draft
> Prepare a research protocol outline for **[context]**. Goal: **[goal]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Use the given facts, label assumptions, and identify information needed to complete the draft.

### Review
> Review this draft for clarity, completeness, feasibility, and unsupported claims: **[draft]**. Rank the gaps by impact and provide a revised draft with open questions clearly marked.

### Tailor
> Tailor this for **[audience/scenario]**, keeping the goal fixed and adapting detail, tone, and examples. Note dependencies, risks, and next actions. Source: **[source material]**.

## Quality checks
- Purpose and scope are clear
- Assumptions and evidence are separated
- Risks and dependencies are considered
- Actions can be verified
- Unknowns remain explicitly marked
