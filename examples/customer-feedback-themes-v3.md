# Customer Feedback Themes Prompt Examples

A feedback themes analysis organizes comments into clear categories while preserving nuance and counterexamples.

## Prompt templates

### Create
> Create a customer feedback themes for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Goal: **[goal]**. Use confirmed information and mark unknowns.

### Review
> Review the supplied material **[material]** for clarity, completeness, consistency, and practical value. Identify the main gaps and recommend specific improvements.

### Follow-up
> Turn the recommendations into a short action plan with role-based owners, dependencies, validation steps, and a review checkpoint.

## Quality checklist
- Goal and audience are clear
- Evidence and assumptions are distinguished
- Risks and limitations are visible
- Actions are specific
- Follow-up is verifiable
