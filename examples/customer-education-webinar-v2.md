# Customer Education Webinar Prompt Examples

A customer education webinar balances a focused learning objective, practical examples, audience questions, and follow-up resources.

## Prompt templates

### Prepare
> Prepare a customer education webinar for **[organization/context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Purpose: **[purpose]**. Use confirmed information, mark assumptions, and call out missing inputs.

### Review
> Review this draft for accuracy, clarity, audience fit, accessibility, and completeness: **[draft]**. Identify risks and give a revised version.

### Follow-up
> Suggest a practical follow-up plan with resources, role-based owners, feedback channels, and ways to evaluate whether the purpose was met. Avoid unsupported outcome claims.

## Checklist
- Purpose and audience are clear
- Claims are accurate and appropriately qualified
- Key steps and responsibilities are explicit
- Risks and open questions are visible
- Follow-up is practical and measurable
