# Service Level Objective Design Prompt Examples

Service level objectives define user-centered reliability targets, measurement windows, and error-budget implications.

## Prompt templates

### Create
> Create a service level objective design for **[team/context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Objective: **[objective]**. Use provided facts, define terms, and identify missing inputs.

### Evaluate
> Evaluate this draft or source material: **[material]**. Find inconsistencies, gaps, ambiguous definitions, and unsupported conclusions. Prioritize fixes and suggest an improved version.

### Apply
> Turn the approved result into an operational checklist. Include responsibilities, review frequency or trigger, exception handling, and evidence needed to confirm completion. Leave unknown values as placeholders.

## Checklist
- Scope and intended users are clear
- Definitions are consistent
- Assumptions and limits are documented
- Responsibilities and exceptions are covered
- Review criteria are actionable
