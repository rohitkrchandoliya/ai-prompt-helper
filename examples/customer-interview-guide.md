# Customer Interview Guide Prompt Examples

## Prepare neutral questions
"Create a customer interview guide to learn about [topic]. Use open-ended, neutral questions that explore real past behavior, current workarounds, pain points, and decision factors. Avoid leading questions and avoid pitching a solution. Audience: [customer segment]."

## Probe an answer
"Suggest neutral follow-up questions for this interview response: [response]. Help uncover context, frequency, consequences, and existing alternatives without putting words in the participant's mouth."

## Summarize interview findings
"Summarize these interview notes into observed behaviors, direct evidence, recurring themes, outliers, and unanswered questions. Distinguish participant statements from researcher interpretation and do not generalize beyond the sample. Notes: [paste]."
