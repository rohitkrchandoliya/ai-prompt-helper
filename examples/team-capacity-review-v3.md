# Team Capacity Review Prompt Examples

A team capacity review compares planned work, available skills, competing priorities, and delivery constraints.

## Prompt templates

### Define needs
> Define a team capacity review for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Desired result: **[result]**. List constraints, inputs, and assumptions.

### Evaluate the draft
> Evaluate this proposal **[draft]** for coverage, clarity, feasibility, edge cases, and risks. Rank gaps and recommend corrections.

### Prepare implementation
> Create a practical implementation checklist with role-based owners, dependencies, validation steps, and open questions. Do not invent missing details.

## Quality checklist
- Users and goals are defined
- Terms and rules are consistent
- Constraints and exceptions are included
- Owners and dependencies are clear
- Validation is possible
