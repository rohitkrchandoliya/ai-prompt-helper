# Customer Adoption Barriers Prompt Examples

An adoption barriers review identifies friction, capability gaps, motivation, and contextual obstacles using evidence.

## Prompt templates

### Design
> Design a customer adoption barriers for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Objective: **[objective]**. Use the supplied facts and state assumptions.

### Evaluate
> Evaluate this draft **[draft]** for usability, completeness, clarity, and potential failure points. List prioritized improvements and explain why they matter.

### Operationalize
> Turn the recommendations into steps with role-based owners, dependencies, validation checks, and unresolved questions.

## Quality checklist
- Objective and audience are explicit
- Unnecessary complexity is avoided
- Assumptions and risks are visible
- Responsibilities are clear
- Follow-up is actionable
