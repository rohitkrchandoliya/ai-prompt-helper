# Content Calendar Prompt Examples

## 1. Plan a content calendar
> Build a content calendar for [audience, goal, channels, cadence]. Include topic, format, purpose, required inputs, and a realistic production timeline. Mark assumptions and avoid inventing events or dates.

## 2. Repurpose existing content
> Suggest channel-appropriate adaptations of this source [content] for [channels]. Preserve factual claims, adjust format and length, and identify what needs a fresh review before publication.

## 3. Check editorial consistency
> Review these planned items [calendar] for repeated topics, gaps, inconsistent messaging, and unrealistic workload. Suggest improvements while respecting the stated cadence and priorities.
