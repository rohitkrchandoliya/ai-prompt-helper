# Product Feedback Prioritization Prompt Examples

A feedback prioritization framework compares user impact, frequency, effort, strategic fit, and evidence quality.

## Prompt templates

### Prepare
> Prepare a product feedback prioritization for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Goal: **[goal]**. Use the supplied information, distinguish facts from assumptions, and list missing inputs.

### Evaluate
> Evaluate the draft or evidence **[material]** for completeness, clarity, feasibility, and risk. Identify gaps, compare reasonable alternatives, and prioritize improvements.

### Put into action
> Turn the recommendations into a practical plan with role-based owners, dependencies, verification steps, and open questions. Do not invent unknown details.

## Quality checklist
- Purpose and audience are clear
- Evidence and assumptions are distinguished
- Risks and constraints are considered
- Recommendations are actionable
- Follow-up can be verified
