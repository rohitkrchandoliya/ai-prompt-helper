# Business Continuity Plan Prompt Examples

## Draft a continuity plan
"Draft a business continuity plan for [team or service]. Cover critical activities, dependencies, minimum operating needs, recovery priorities, communications, alternate procedures, and responsible roles. Label assumptions and request confirmation of recovery targets."

## Analyze disruption scenarios
"Evaluate plausible disruptions to [operation], including system outage, staff unavailability, supplier interruption, and facility access loss. For each, describe impact, early signals, workaround, escalation, and evidence needed to validate the plan."

## Run a tabletop exercise
"Design a tabletop exercise for [scenario] with injects, participant roles, decision points, evaluation criteria, and debrief questions. Avoid assuming the organization has capabilities not described in the supplied context."
