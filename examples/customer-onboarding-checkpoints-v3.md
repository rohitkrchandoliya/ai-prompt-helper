# Customer Onboarding Checkpoints Prompt Examples

Onboarding checkpoints help teams verify that customers understand key tasks, have access, and know where to get help.

## Prompt templates

### Draft
> Draft a customer onboarding checkpoints for **[team/context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Intended outcome: **[outcome]**. Use supplied facts and flag missing details.

### Review
> Review this draft for clarity, inclusion, feasibility, and completeness: **[draft]**. Identify confusing expectations and recommend practical improvements.

### Put into practice
> Turn the agreed approach into a short checklist with responsible roles, review points, exception handling, and observable signs that it is working.

## Quality checklist
- Purpose and audience are clear
- Expectations are realistic and specific
- Exceptions and open questions are noted
- Responsibilities are explicit
- Review criteria are observable
