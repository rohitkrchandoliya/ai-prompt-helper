# Customer Journey Experiment Prompt Examples

A journey experiment tests a focused improvement at a defined point in the customer experience.

## Prompt templates

### Create
> Create a customer journey experiment for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Goal: **[goal]**. Base the result on provided facts and label uncertainty.

### Assess
> Assess this proposal or evidence: **[material]**. Identify strengths, gaps, risks, dependencies, and alternative explanations. Rank improvements by impact and feasibility.

### Deliver
> Convert the recommended approach into steps, responsible roles, verification methods, and review checkpoints. Keep unknown details as placeholders.

## Quality checklist
- Goal and scope are explicit
- Evidence is traceable
- Assumptions and risks are visible
- Steps are actionable
- Results can be evaluated
