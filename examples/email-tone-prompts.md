# Professional Email Tone Prompt Examples

## 1. Make an email concise
> Edit this email [draft] for clarity and brevity. Preserve the facts, requested action, deadlines, and intended tone; do not add commitments or claims.

## 2. Adjust tone
> Rewrite this message [draft] to sound [warm/direct/diplomatic/formal] for [recipient/context]. Keep the core message intact and avoid making it overly apologetic or aggressive.

## 3. Draft a follow-up
> Based on this prior exchange [context], draft a polite follow-up asking about [specific item]. Do not imply a deadline or prior agreement unless the context states one.
