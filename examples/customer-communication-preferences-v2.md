# Customer Communication Preferences Prompt Examples

A communication preference plan records audience needs, channel suitability, consent, and frequency expectations.

## Prompt templates

### Prepare
> Prepare a customer communication preferences for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Goal: **[goal]**. Respect privacy and other stated constraints, use only supplied facts, and flag uncertainty.

### Analyze
> Analyze the provided material: **[material]**. Identify themes, gaps, dependencies, risks, and alternative interpretations. Avoid conclusions unsupported by the evidence.

### Recommend
> Recommend practical next steps with responsible roles, dependencies, safeguards, and review criteria. State which questions must be answered before decisions are made.

## Quality checklist
- Purpose and scope are clear
- Sensitive details are handled appropriately
- Evidence and interpretation are separated
- Dependencies and risks are visible
- Next steps are verifiable
