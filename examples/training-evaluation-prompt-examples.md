# Training Evaluation Framework Prompt Examples

A training evaluation framework measures learning and application using proportionate methods and clear limitations.

## Prompt templates

### Design
> Design a training evaluation framework for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Desired outcome: **[outcome]**. State required inputs and assumptions, and organize the result into steps and measurable checks.

### Review
> Review this proposed plan: **[draft]**. Identify missing elements, unclear terms, feasibility concerns, and risks. Suggest prioritized revisions and provide a cleaner version.

### Operationalize
> Turn the approved plan into a usable guide for **[team]**. Include sequence, responsible roles, required inputs, exceptions, and a method to confirm completion. Keep unknown details as placeholders.

## Quality checklist
- Scope and audience are explicit
- Inputs and responsibilities are defined
- Risks and exceptions are addressed
- Success criteria are measurable where possible
- Review process is clear
