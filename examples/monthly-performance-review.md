# Monthly Performance Review Prompt Examples

## Summarize performance
"Summarize this month's performance against the stated goals. Include actuals, targets, trends, notable changes, explanations supported by evidence, and areas needing investigation. Separate measured results from hypotheses. Data: [paste]."

## Explain variances
"Analyze differences between this month and the prior period or target. Calculate absolute and percentage differences where possible, note seasonality or data limitations, and avoid asserting causes without evidence."

## Build next month's actions
"Turn the review findings into a short action plan with priority, intended outcome, owner role, due date to confirm, dependencies, and measure of success. Distinguish proposed actions from approved commitments."
