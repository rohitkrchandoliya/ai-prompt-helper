# Customer Feedback Survey Prompt Examples

A customer feedback survey uses focused questions, appropriate response formats, and clear plans for interpreting results.

## Prompt templates

### Design
> Design a customer feedback survey for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Desired outcome: **[outcome]**. Use provided constraints and clearly flag unknowns.

### Analyze
> Analyze these responses or records: **[material]**. Summarize patterns, outliers, limitations, and alternative explanations. Avoid claiming causation without evidence.

### Improve
> Based on the analysis, recommend prioritized improvements, responsible roles, dependencies, and a way to check whether the changes help.

## Quality checklist
- Purpose and audience are clear
- Questions or criteria are unbiased and relevant
- Limitations are explicit
- Findings are evidence-based
- Follow-up is actionable
