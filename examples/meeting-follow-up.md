# Meeting Follow-Up Prompt Examples

## Extract action items
"Turn these meeting notes into an action list with task, owner only when explicitly named, due date only when explicitly stated, dependency, and next step. Separate decisions from suggestions and flag missing ownership. Notes: [paste]."

## Draft a recap
"Write a concise meeting recap with purpose, decisions, key discussion points, open questions, and action items. Preserve nuance where there was disagreement and do not present tentative ideas as final decisions. Notes: [paste]."

## Track unresolved items
"Create a follow-up tracker from these notes. Group items into decisions needed, actions, risks, and information requests. Include status only if supported by the notes and identify what needs confirmation. Notes: [paste]."
