# Customer Value Review Meeting Prompt Examples

A customer value review meeting aligns progress evidence, blockers, agreed outcomes, and next actions.

## Prompt templates

### Prepare
> Prepare a customer value review meeting for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Goal: **[goal]**. Use supplied details and identify missing information.

### Review
> Review this draft or set of notes **[material]** for clarity, coverage, edge cases, and practical feasibility. Rank gaps and recommend corrections.

### Turn into actions
> Create an action plan with responsible roles, dependencies, verification steps, and unresolved questions. Do not invent facts.

## Quality checklist
- Goal and scope are clear
- Criteria are explicit
- Risks and exceptions are considered
- Responsibilities are assigned by role
- Completion can be verified
