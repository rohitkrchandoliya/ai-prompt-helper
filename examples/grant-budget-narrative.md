# Grant Budget Narrative Prompt Examples

## Explain budget line items
"Draft a budget narrative from the supplied budget table. For each line item, explain its purpose, calculation basis, and connection to project activities. Flag unsupported amounts and do not create figures that are not provided. Budget: [paste]."

## Check budget consistency
"Compare the budget narrative with the budget table and project plan. Identify inconsistent amounts, mismatched periods, unclear allocations, and activities without funding. Show the source of each discrepancy and questions for the budget owner."

## Prepare assumptions
"Create a budget assumptions section listing unit costs, quantities, time periods, allocation methods, and exclusions from the supplied inputs. Mark estimates as estimates and list supporting documentation still needed."
