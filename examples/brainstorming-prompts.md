# Brainstorming Prompt Examples

## Generate options
Suggest 12 distinct ideas for [goal] within these constraints: [constraints]. Avoid near-duplicates and group ideas by approach.

## Expand one idea
Develop this idea into a practical concept with audience, value, required resources, and first steps: [idea].

## Evaluate ideas
Compare these ideas against the stated criteria. Note trade-offs and assumptions instead of declaring a universal winner: [ideas and criteria].
