# Research Source Evaluation Prompt Examples

Use these prompts to assess source quality before relying on a claim.

## Evaluate a source
"Evaluate this source for author expertise, publication venue, evidence quality, recency, potential conflicts of interest, and relevance to my question. Separate verifiable facts from interpretation. Identify what cannot be established from the supplied material. Source: [paste source or details]. Question: [research question]."

## Compare sources
"Compare these sources on methodology, evidence, date, perspective, and limitations. Note where they agree or conflict, and what additional evidence would resolve the disagreement. Do not assume either source is correct solely because of its reputation. Sources: [paste]."

## Verify a claim
"Break this claim into checkable subclaims. For each, list the evidence needed, what evidence is currently supplied, and what remains unverified. Do not invent citations or imply that a source was checked when it was not. Claim: [claim]."
