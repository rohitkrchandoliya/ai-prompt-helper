# Customer Value Realization Prompt Examples

A customer value realization plan connects the intended outcomes of a product or service to evidence, adoption milestones, and review conversations.

## Prompt templates

### Build a plan
> Create a customer value realization plan for **[customer/context]**. Desired outcomes: **[outcomes]**. Current situation: **[baseline]**. Use supplied facts, flag missing data, and propose realistic milestones and evidence.

### Review progress
> Review the current progress notes: **[notes]**. Compare stated outcomes with available evidence, identify blockers and assumptions, and suggest practical corrective actions.

### Prepare a value review
> Create a concise review agenda for **[customer/team]** covering goals, progress, evidence, unresolved gaps, next steps, and owners. Do not claim outcomes that have not been verified.

## Quality checklist
- Outcomes are specific and agreed
- Baselines and evidence sources are documented
- Assumptions and blockers are visible
- Milestones have responsible roles
- Next steps are measurable and realistic
