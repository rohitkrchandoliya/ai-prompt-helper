# Research Prompt Examples

## Compare options
Compare [option A], [option B], and [option C] for [use case]. Define decision criteria first, summarize trade-offs in a table, identify which facts need current verification, and state what would change the recommendation.

## Evaluate a claim
Assess this claim: [claim]. Identify the evidence needed to support or refute it, distinguish primary from secondary sources, and explain uncertainty. Do not invent citations or treat absence of evidence as proof.

## Summarize a topic
Give me a structured overview of [topic] for [audience]. Cover established findings, active disagreements, limitations, and open questions. Label facts, interpretations, and unknowns separately.
