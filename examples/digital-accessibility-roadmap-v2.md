# Digital Accessibility Roadmap Prompt Examples

A digital accessibility roadmap prioritizes barriers, affected users, remediation, and repeatable validation.

## Prompt templates

### Prepare
> Prepare a digital accessibility roadmap for **[context]**. Audience: **[audience]**. Objective: **[objective]**. Use the provided facts, identify unknowns, and make recommendations actionable.

### Evaluate
> Evaluate the draft or current approach: **[details]**. Identify gaps, risks, alternative approaches, and evidence needed. Rank recommendations by impact and feasibility.

### Implement
> Convert the selected recommendations into a plan with tasks, responsible roles, dependencies, review points, and clear completion checks.

## Quality checklist
- Goal and audience are explicit
- Claims are evidence-based
- Constraints and risks are considered
- Responsibilities are clear
- Success criteria can be checked
