# Project Risk Register Prompt Examples

## Create an initial register
"Create an initial risk register for [project]. Include risk statement, cause, potential impact, likelihood, severity, early warning sign, mitigation, contingency, owner role, and review date. Label estimates as preliminary and call out missing information."

## Challenge assumptions
"Review this project plan and identify hidden assumptions, dependencies, external constraints, and failure modes. Convert each credible concern into a specific risk with a practical mitigation. Plan: [paste]."

## Prepare a risk review
"Prepare a short risk-review agenda focused on changes since the last review, top risks, mitigation owners, overdue actions, and decisions needed. Do not invent status updates or claim risks are resolved without evidence."
