# Supplier Performance Review Prompt Examples

## Review supplier performance
"Assess this supplier's performance against the agreed service levels and deliverables. Summarize measured results, misses, trends, evidence, and open questions. Separate documented facts from explanations that require supplier confirmation. Data: [paste]."

## Prepare a review meeting
"Create a supplier review agenda covering outcomes, service issues, corrective actions, upcoming demand, risks, and decisions. Include evidence to bring, responsible roles, and a clear meeting outcome."

## Create a corrective action plan
"Turn the confirmed performance gaps into corrective actions with issue, impact, root-cause status, action owner role, target date to agree, success measure, and verification method. Do not label a cause confirmed unless evidence supports it."
