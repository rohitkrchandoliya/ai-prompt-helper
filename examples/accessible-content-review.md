# Accessible Content Review Prompt Examples

## Review plain language
"Review this content for plain language, clear headings, descriptive links, concise instructions, and understandable error messages. Preserve necessary technical accuracy and identify terms that need explanation. Content: [paste]."

## Improve inclusive instructions
"Rewrite these instructions to be direct, respectful, and usable by a broad audience. Avoid assumptions about ability, device, location, or prior knowledge. Preserve required steps and flag missing information. Instructions: [paste]."

## Check image descriptions
"Suggest concise alt text for the described image based on its purpose in context. Include meaningful information and avoid repeating nearby text. If the image itself is not supplied or its content is uncertain, ask for it instead of guessing. Context: [paste]."
