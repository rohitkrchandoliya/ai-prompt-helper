# Operations Checklist

## Prompt template

> Help me create a operations checklist using the information I provide. Ask concise questions when key details are missing, organize the output clearly, and include practical next steps.

## Context to include
- Purpose and audience
- Relevant background and constraints
- Available source material
- Preferred format and timing

## Quality checklist
- Is the output clear and specific?
- Are unknowns and assumptions identified?
- Are next steps concrete?
