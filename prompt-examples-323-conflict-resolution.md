# Conflict Resolution Prompt Examples

Prompts to support conflict resolution with clear context, practical outputs, explicit assumptions, and actionable next steps.

## Reusable prompt

> Help me create a conflict resolution tailored to the context below. Ask concise clarifying questions if key information is missing. Use only the facts provided, label assumptions, structure the response clearly, and finish with actionable next steps.

## Context to provide
- Goal and audience
- Relevant background and constraints
- Available evidence or source material
- Desired format and length
- Deadline, owner, or success criteria where relevant

## Quality checklist
- Is the output specific to the supplied context?
- Are facts, assumptions, and unknowns distinguished?
- Are recommendations practical and verifiable?
- Are relevant risks and stakeholder impacts considered?
