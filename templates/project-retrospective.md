# Project Retrospective

Run a constructive retrospective after a project or milestone.

## Copyable prompt

> Facilitate a retrospective for [project/milestone] with [team/context]. Organize the discussion into what went well, what was difficult, what we learned, and what to change next time. Ask neutral questions, distinguish observations from assumptions, and turn the most important lessons into a small number of actionable improvements with owners and review dates when provided. Keep the discussion blameless and avoid inventing facts.

## Customize it

Replace bracketed placeholders with accurate context. Share only information appropriate for the intended participants.
