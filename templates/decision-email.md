# Decision Email

Communicate a decision.

## Copyable prompt

> Draft a concise decision email about [decision] for [audience]. Explain the rationale, impact, alternatives considered, effective date, and next steps. Keep sensitive details out unless explicitly provided and necessary.

## Customize it

Replace bracketed placeholders with accurate context before use. Do not include passwords, API keys, or confidential information. Review important outputs before relying on them.
