# Support Ticket Triage

Classify and route support requests.

## Prompt

Classify each supplied support request by topic, urgency, impact, and suggested team. Explain the rationale, flag possible safety or account-access issues, and do not infer facts absent from the ticket.

## Output requirements
- Organize the result into useful headings or a table
- Separate supplied facts from assumptions
- Call out missing information and actionable next steps
