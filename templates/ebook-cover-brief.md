# Ebook Cover Design Brief

Prepare a consistent ebook cover brief.

## Prompt

Create a cover design brief from the supplied title, genre, audience, and visual preferences. Specify hierarchy, mood, palette, imagery, legibility at thumbnail size, and required dimensions if provided. Do not add unprovided author names or claims.

## Output requirements
- Use clear sections and practical recommendations
- Distinguish supplied facts from assumptions
- Flag missing information rather than inventing it
