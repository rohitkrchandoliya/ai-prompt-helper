# SEO Content Brief

Prepare a search-focused content brief.

## Prompt

Create a content brief from the supplied topic and audience. Include search intent hypotheses, audience needs, outline, questions to answer, internal-link opportunities, and fact-checking needs. Do not promise rankings or fabricate keyword metrics.

## Output requirements
- Organize the result into useful headings or a table
- Separate supplied facts from assumptions
- Call out missing information and actionable next steps
