# Sales Call Preparation

Prepare for a discovery call.

## Prompt

Prepare a discovery-call brief from the supplied account context. Include likely goals, open-ended questions, potential fit signals, objections to explore, and next steps. Do not invent facts about the prospect.

## Output requirements
- Organize the result into useful headings or a table
- Separate supplied facts from assumptions
- Call out missing information and actionable next steps
