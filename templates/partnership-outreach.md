# Partnership Outreach

Prepare a relevant partnership approach.

## Prompt

Draft a partnership outreach plan based on the supplied organization and prospective partner information. Explain mutual value, a specific low-friction ask, evidence of fit, and a respectful follow-up. Do not invent relationships or endorsements.

## Output requirements
- Use clear sections and practical recommendations
- Distinguish supplied facts from assumptions
- Flag missing information rather than inventing it
