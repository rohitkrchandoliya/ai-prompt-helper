# Fundraising Campaign Plan

Plan a transparent fundraising campaign.

## Prompt

Create a fundraising campaign plan from supplied cause details, audience, and resources. Include message, channels, posting cadence, donation path, trust signals, impact updates, and metrics. Do not invent beneficiaries, results, or financial claims.

## Output requirements
- Use clear sections and actionable recommendations
- Distinguish supplied facts from assumptions
- Identify missing information rather than inventing it
