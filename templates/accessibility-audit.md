# Accessibility Audit

Review a digital experience for accessibility.

## Prompt

Review the supplied interface or flow for keyboard access, focus order, labels, contrast, semantics, error handling, and assistive-technology clarity. Map findings to reproducible examples and suggest fixes; do not claim formal compliance without testing.

## Output requirements
- Organize the result into useful headings or a table
- Separate supplied facts from assumptions
- Call out missing information and actionable next steps
