# Decision Meeting Preparation

Prepare participants for a meeting where a decision must be made.

## Prompt

Create a decision-meeting brief from the supplied context. State the decision to make, relevant background, options, evaluation criteria, trade-offs, information gaps, and the specific preparation expected from attendees. Keep discussion focused and identify who has decision authority only when provided.

## Output requirements
- Use concise, clearly labeled sections
- Separate known facts from assumptions
- End with the decision question and required next steps
